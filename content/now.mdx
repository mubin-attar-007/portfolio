## Building now

- **CareLTC**: NFC room rounds for long-term care, with the iOS and Android app, the admin portal and the API with its scheduler. I lead it, still maintain it and ship new features. [Read the CareLTC case study](/work/careltc).
- **Bench Discovery**: a web platform where legal teams review document sets with answers that cite the page, and a Windows desktop app that triages and redacts documents on the PC. I designed, built and maintain both. [Read the Bench Discovery case study](/work/bench-discovery).
- **This site, redesigned**: every page rebuilt to a product standard, and the "Ask this site" assistant evaluated against a golden set of questions, each answer scored on the pages it cites, the facts it states and whether every number it gives is in its sources.
- **A Hinglish voice agent**: a new public project, in progress. It changes a delivery slot over a live call, in Hindi and English mixed, and is measured on whether it ever double-books or confirms a change the order system does not show.
- **Public projects**: [DBWhisper](/work/dbwhisper), with public source and a case study, and [TradePulse](/work/tradepulse), now archived. Measured results, each with its method, are on [Evals](/evals).

## Reading

- The **Model Context Protocol** specification: typed tool-use as a protocol, not per-agent glue.
- **Spider** (Yu et al., 2018): the cross-domain text-to-SQL benchmark I measured DBWhisper against.
- **Agent memory beyond flat conversation summaries**: what actually survives a long session, and what should.
