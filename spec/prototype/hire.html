A note to hiring teams

Most AI projects stall between the demo and the customer's real environment. That gap is where I've worked since 2024, and it's mostly made of questions like these:

Records can't leave the PC?
Tests over the compiled code prove they don't.
Staff share phones on the floor?
Floor-phone accounts, and lock screens that show room numbers only.
Old app builds stay installed?
New API fields are opt-in, so older builds keep working.
A model writes the SQL?
A deterministic validator decides whether it runs.
A result fails its own audit?
It's retired in public, with the reason.

I've done this on small teams, working directly with the staff who use the software. I direct AI coding agents for much of the implementation. I set the architecture, the quality gates and the invariants, review every change and own the releases.

I'm looking for a forward deployed or applied AI role where that is the job: sitting with customers, writing down their constraints, and shipping systems they rely on.
